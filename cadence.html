Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Cadence Labs, Inc. ("Cadence," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, password (hashed) when you sign up.
  • Workspace data: projects, tasks, goals, and content you create.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, device/browser info, IP address.
  • Billing data: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How We Use Data

  • To provide and improve the service.
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, security alerts).
  • To analyze usage and fix bugs.
  • To prevent abuse and secure the platform.

Data Sharing

We share data only with subprocessors that power the service (Supabase for database/auth, Stripe for payments, email providers for delivery). We never sell your data to third parties.

Your Rights

  • Access: request a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: request account and data deletion.
  • Portability: export your data anytime from the app.
  • Opt-out: unsubscribe from marketing emails anytime.

To exercise any right, email privacy@cadence.io. We respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and analytics cookies to understand usage. You can disable analytics cookies in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@cadence.io or write to: Cadence Labs, Lagos, Nigeria.

Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: July 2026

This DPA applies to customers who use Cadence to process personal data subject to GDPR or similar regulations.

1. Roles

Cadence acts as a Data Processor. The customer acts as Data Controller. Cadence processes personal data only on documented instructions from the customer.

2. Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Row-level security on all user data.
  • SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure.
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing.
  • Access controls with audit logging.

3. Subprocessors

Cadence uses: Supabase (database, auth, hosting, Ireland/EU), Stripe (payments, USA), and email delivery providers. We notify customers 30 days before adding a new subprocessor.

4. Data Subject Rights

Cadence assists customers in fulfilling data subject requests (access, rectification, deletion, portability) via data export tools and API.

5. Breach Notification

Cadence notifies customers of any personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware, with details and remediation steps.

6. Data Deletion

On account termination, Cadence deletes all customer data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Contact

Email dpa@cadence.io for questions or to execute a signed DPA.
